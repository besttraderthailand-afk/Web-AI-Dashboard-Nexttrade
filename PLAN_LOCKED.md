# Locked commission plan (2026-10-02)

Package is 20 USDT, integer, matched by bill number.

- Tier 15% / 10% / 5% of the package, paid immediately, including repurchase. Cap and EA/AI rental pay no tier.
- Unilevel is 21% of the bill, 10 pay slots of 0.42 on a 20 USDT bill. Walk the sponsor line, skip inactive nodes for that bill only, and do not move the node. If the active line is shorter than 10, the empty slots stay with the company.
- Pool 12% is reserved from packages and services. Rank is recalculated from this month's volume only. Floors: Bronze 200, Silver 1,000, Gold 5,000, Diamond 20,000, Legend 80,000. Each member is paid only their own rank bucket, divided by the number of members at that same rank. Diamond does not also receive Bronze, Silver, or Gold. A drop from Diamond to Gold pays the Gold bucket only. Below 200 pays nothing that round.
- Leadership 8% of the package bill only when 10-level package volume crosses 200, then 400, 800, 1,600, and so on. Other bills hold the 8% in the company share.

Company remainder on a 20 USDT package: 5.80 if leadership unlocks on that bill, 7.40 if it does not. Cap and EA/AI pay unilevel 21%, pool 12%, and leadership 8% on an unlock bill, and no tier. On 5 USDT that is company 3.35, or 2.95 if leadership unlocks.
